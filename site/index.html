How it works

Three steps, about two minutes after the round

1

Mark the card

One short line per hole, on any course's scorecard or the printable pocket sheet. Its QR code opens the guide on your phone.

2

Type it in at home

Add the course once (par and yardage), then enter the round. Validation catches impossible combinations before they skew your stats.

3

Practice what matters

See strokes gained by category and distance, trends over 5, 20 or all rounds, and a ranked list of what to practice next.

The card

One line per hole, on any scorecard

Always in the same order: Score · Drive · Approach · Green · Short game · Putts · Penalties. Skip anything that didn't happen.

Features

Tour-grade analysis, paper-card effort

📊

Four-category strokes gained

Off the tee, approach, around the green and putting, per hole, per round and per 18 holes. Categories always add up to your score.

🎯

Practice priorities

Ranked by strokes lost, each pointing at your weakest distance or lie, and only once there are enough samples to trust.

📏

Distance and lie breakdowns

Approach by distance band, short game by lie, putting by first-putt distance, and approach miss patterns.

📶

Works with no signal

An installable web app that caches itself. Enter rounds and see stats on a phone with no reception.

🔒

Private by default

No tracking, and an account is optional. Rounds are stored on your device; sign in with Google to keep them across devices, or download your data any time.

⚖️

Choose your benchmark

Compare against PGA Tour players or an estimated scratch golfer. Handicap-matched baselines are on the roadmap.

Architecture

Local-first, static, and secure by default

The app is a static web app. All data and calculations stay on the device, so there is no server or database to run or secure in Phase 1. Full detail is in the technical design.

Strokes-gained model

Each recorded position (tee, approach, short game, first putt, holed) is looked up in an expected-strokes table by distance and lie.

SG(shot) = E(start) − E(end) − strokes
OTT + APP + ARG + PUTT = E(tee) − score

Baselines: PGA Tour (Broadie, Every Shot Counts) and an estimated scratch baseline (ADR 0008). A property test checks the sum invariant for every baseline.

Security posture

  • Signed out, nothing leaves the device; signed in, data is scoped to your Google ID
  • Strict CSP, HSTS, frame-ancestors none
  • Private S3 behind CloudFront OAC
  • GitHub OIDC, per-environment roles
  • Dev can't modify prod resources
  • Prod deploys need approval
  • SHA-pinned actions, least privilege
  • SBOM, provenance, keyless signing
